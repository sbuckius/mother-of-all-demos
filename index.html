About the Performance
An Immersive Multimedia Experience

Step into a zany, immersive, live, multimedia performance featuring a female tech CEO who embodies female-coded robots, memorializes patents by women for textiles and computation devices, performs AI-generated instructions for mechanized domestic labor, trains Mechanical Turks in DEI tasks, babysits unruly AI bots, includes the 1870 article by Matilda Gage, "Woman as Inventor," and broadcasts the audio of Harlow Shapley, the "kilo-girls" fabricator.

This interactive experience—blending irony and sincerity, epic blunders and meager successes, chaotic messes and illogical order—will leave you questioning power structures in technology, past and present, pondering the Matilda Effect—the ongoing erasure and undervaluing of women's contributions in tech, and yearning for technology made with CARE for humans and the world.

Bring It to Your Venue
Host This Performance
View the Tech Pack ▼

The following categories describe what is desired from the venue, what is optional, and what the artist brings.

Space & Stage

  • Stage or space that is about 8 ft by 16 ft
  • Space on stage for pink domestic objects (minimum 4 ft x 4 ft)
  • Space for a small pink desk that is 24 inches by 24 inches footprint
  • Floor that I can place pink gaffer tape

A/V & Technology

  • Podium with computer hook up to one projector or monitor and microphone to speakers. HDMI connection.
  • 2 monitors or 2 projectors with different feeds from 2 different laptops. Only one of these should have sound output that will come from a video playing on one of the computers. I supply the laptops.
  • HDMI connection to both monitors
  • Microphone for me to speak into
  • Reliable wifi that I can connect to by phone and computer

Audience

  • Audience seating
  • Walkway into audience for me to pay the audience members
  • Distribution of badges. Can be on a table where I place pink baskets or can be on the chairs.

Timing

  • Duration of performance as is: 45 minutes. 15 minutes Q & A (optional).

Optional

  • 1 or 2 metal sign holders for 22 x 28 sign. I have the printed sign.
  • 2 snake plants
  • Pink lights that shine up and hit the podium

Items That I Bring

  • Costumes
  • Pink desk
  • 2 computers
  • iPhone and head mount
  • Pink domestic objects
  • Pink gaffer tape
  • Pink money box with pennies
